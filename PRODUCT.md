# Product brief — Morea (Evidence Pack)

## Name

Morea

## One-liner

Educational EU AI Act **Article 4** literacy trail → exportable Evidence Pack. **Not** a compliance service.

## Audience

Learners exploring AI Act literacy for small professional firms (illustrative).

## Job to be done

Complete the trail (inventory → assess → module → quiz → optional policy → pack) and export/verify a pack.

## Surface types

- Marketing / landing: light Persuade with educational framing
- App screens: multi-step literacy flow (Operate)

## Constraints

- Stack: plain HTML/JS + serverless; optional Neon; Mistral for module
- Live: morea-pack.vercel.app (not morea.vercel.app — third party)
- Hiring: **secondary** to GRYPS
- Commercial status: non-commercial educational demo

## Honesty

Never claim legal advice, certification, or “get compliant for your company.” Packs are illustrative.

## Success look

Clear steps; Evidence Pack is the demo object; mulberry/evidence brand; non-commercial line visible.
