Article 4 & Morea, answered

What is Article 4 of the EU AI Act?

Providers and deployers must take measures to support the development of AI literacy among staff and others using AI on their behalf, taking account of role and context. The Digital Omnibus on AI (mid-2026) clarified that this does not require guaranteeing any specific literacy level for any individual. Educational summary only — not legal advice.

When did Article 4 take effect?

2 February 2025. Omnibus amendments to the wording entered into force mid-2026; the duty to take literacy measures remains.

Is Morea a compliance product?

No. It is an educational / illustrative research prototype for practising documentation habits — not a conformity assessment or legal advice. No accounts / no billing.

How is this different from learning how LLMs work?

Understanding model mechanics is valuable. Morea practices a different job: documenting literacy measures and AI use in a small-team Evidence Pack format.
